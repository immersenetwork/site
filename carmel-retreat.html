Carmel Retreat

Get away. Be refreshed.
Encounter the Spirit.

Each year, leaders from across the Immerse family gather for the Carmel Retreat — a set-apart time to slow down, be renewed in the Holy Spirit, and journey together. Expect a few days of teaching, ministry and rest: exploring the spiritual battle, conquering fears, praying prophetically, and learning to rest and “tend the wells.”

What to expect

A few days set apart

1

Teaching & Impartation

Spirit-filled teaching with an emphasis on the five-fold ministry, equipping leaders to carry Spirit life back to their churches.

2

Rest & Refreshing

Space to breathe, pray and be renewed — time to rest and “tend the wells” so leaders return replenished, not depleted.

3

Prophetic & Freedom Ministry

Praying prophetically together, with ministry for deliverance and freedom — unblocking the “wells” in people’s lives.

4

Family & Connection

Deepening relationships across the network — the kind of genuine spiritual connection that Immerse is built on.
