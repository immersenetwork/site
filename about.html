Our History

A Spirit-filled Journey

Immerse's began in 2007, when a couple of pastors and church leaders decided to meet together for a time of teaching, ministry and refreshing. Originally called Isaiah 58, those who were part of it carried a genuine desire to experience the freedom and life that comes with chasing after the fullness of what Jesus paid for on the cross. The gathering was such a meaningful time that it became an annual conference where leaders came to receive, and to minister to one another.

As the number of pastors attending grew, the name eventually changed to Immerse, which is rooted in the intention to be immersed in the Holy Spirit and to live that out through every facet of everyday life. Today, Immerse is a movement of like-minded pastors, church leaders and churches, with many ministry and social gatherings across South Africa, including recent connections with churches in the United Kingdom.

Our Approach

How we journey together

1

Developing Relationships

Developing strategic relationships with key leaders, then leadership teams, and ultimately with local churches — fostering genuine spiritual connection within communities.

2

Creating Environments

Creating environments in which we teach on and demonstrate Spirit life, with an emphasis on five-fold ministry and reciprocal spiritual impartation.

3

Developing Material

Producing resources that express our core teaching — including a teaching guide that articulates, inspires and enables people and churches to enter the Immerse pattern.

4

Ministry Opportunities

Yearly conferences, inner healing, deliverance and prophetic ministry — unblocking "wells" in people's lives — and worship-team development that facilitates Spirit-led worship.
